    About.

    Engineer by training, technical artist by trade, generalist by interest. The throughline is being the person who can carry a project across the boundaries — from concept to shader to deployment, from API to algorithm to design.

    An engineering-school path — DUT GEII at IUT Nice, CPGE ATS in Paris, two years at ESTP — then game programming at Isart Digital, and largely self-taught since. The first proper engagements were as Technical Artist — six months at Little Red Dog Games shipping Calico Inferno's 70-shader URP library, then six at SABDA on immersive 360° exhibitions.

    As a freelance technical artist, the standout client engagement is a WebGL 360° tourism campaign for a Gulf tourism board (via a Doha agency). Alongside it, a deep body of self-directed R&D — a WebGPU multiplayer, a quantitative-trading framework, a rooftop-solar modelling study, a 42-page legal site at Lighthouse 95+, and ongoing engine, ML, and procedural work.

    Engineering for the parts that need rigour; craft for the parts that need taste. Comfortable as the only technical voice in the room or as one of many. Based in Nice; registered as a freelance entity in France, available worldwide.
