# HTML → PDF utility

A one-off utility. Reads a styled HTML report and produces a PDF; re-run after edits and the PDF regenerates byte-for-byte. Not a reusable tool so much as a **reproducible artefact** — the source-of-truth is HTML, not the output PDF.

## Where it stands

Functional for its specific use. Low reusability, high task-completion value.
